# MARPOL Annex I Overview

## What is MARPOL?

MARPOL (International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships) is the main international convention covering prevention of pollution of the marine environment by ships.

**Annex I** covers regulations for the prevention of pollution by oil.

## Oil Record Book Requirements

Every oil tanker of 150 GT and above and every ship of 400 GT and above shall be provided with an Oil Record Book.

### Part I — Machinery Space Operations
Required for every ship of 400 GT and above.

Operations to be recorded:
- **Code A** — Ballasting of fuel oil tanks
- **Code B** — Cleaning of fuel oil tanks
- **Code C** — Discharge of dirty ballast
- **Code D** — Cleaning of bilge water
- **Code E** — Discharge of bilge water
- **Code F** — Condition of OWS and ODM equipment
- **Code G** — Accidental or other exceptional discharge
- **Code H** — Bunkering of fuel or bulk lubricating oil
- **Code I** — Additional operational procedures and general remarks

### Part II — Cargo/Ballast Operations
Required for oil tankers of 150 GT and above.

Operations to be recorded:
- **Code A** — Loading of oil cargo
- **Code B** — Internal transfer of oil cargo during voyage
- **Code C** — Unloading of oil cargo
- **Code D** — Ballasting of cargo tanks and dedicated clean ballast tanks
- **Code E** — Cleaning of cargo tanks including crude oil washing
- **Code F** — Discharge of ballast water
- **Code G** — Accidental or other exceptional discharge

## Retention Requirements

The Oil Record Book shall be kept on board and available for inspection for a period of 3 years after the last entry.
